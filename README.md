Hi there! 👋

Here's what you can expect from me as an Engineering Manager, should we end up in the same team.

If you're wondering, here's [my personality](https://www.16personalities.com/profiles/ccbff1c627e23).

## TL;DR

> ✅ Make sure the team is happy, efficient, and working on the right
> things to support the company's growth.

## Not TL;DR

Let me give you some details about that, so let's start with the basics:

> **"You're not wasting my time"**
>
> **"You should expect me to have your back 100% of the time."**

If in doubt, those two sentences should give you most of the answers
when it comes to your EM. I can only promise two things:

-   **I will make mistakes.** I might even don't realize I'm making a
    mistake. Please tell me if so 🙏. I value honest feedback.
-   **I will do my best.** That's the least I can do for the team. I
    believe managers work for their direct reports.

The main missions are:

-   Make sure **the team is happy**. I truly believe you cannot perform
    and deliver high quality work if you're not happy at work and in
    your life. Happy individuals is the right path to a happy team and
    successful collaboration.
-   Make the team efficient. Provide the right tool and enablers to make
    sure you can do your work smoothly.
-   Make sure we are working on the right things at the right moment, to
    support the company strategy and that everybody in the team is
    aligned and pushing in the same direction.
-   Make sure we execute well. It is not enough to build a wonderful but
    useless system. We have to get things done.
-   Ensure high quality delivery, to support the company's growth and have
    the highest availability and reliability.
-   Make sure our team is getting what we need from other teams, and
    that other teams are getting what they need from us.

### You know what you're doing

I am not here to help you. You know your job, that's why you're here. I
can show you the way and accompany you, but you're the one who can help
yourself. Struggling with something? Let's have a chat about it. Don't
know what to prioritize? Let's have a chat about it. Something's
bothering you? Let's... you know the drill. Don't wait for our 1-1 to
talk. The sooner we discuss the issue the better.

To go even further, I am not the official decision maker in the team. I
am accountable for the team's decisions, even if I'm not actually the
one making them. I will probably rely on you as you have the full
picture and context. I might challenge the decision to make sure we're
going the direction though. If you're uncomfortable to make a decision
and ask me to make one, I'll do it.

### Feedback

If you have feedback for me, whether it's good or bad, please give it.
It can be something you liked and that we should do more often,
something you thought I could have done better, something you thought I
totally messed up, or anything else. Even if you think it might not be
the case, I really do want to hear it. And if you think I don't want to
hear it, I'd love to hear why you feel that way.

If you can give me this feedback face-to-face (Meet or in person during
on site days), that's what I'd prefer. If you're uncomfortable with
that, shoot an email or a Slack message, I would rather you do that than
not saying anything at all.

If you're uncomfortable giving me some feedback yourself, it is totally
OK to give it to someone above me in the management chain so they can
anonymously relay it to me.

On top of that, if you have feedback for a team member or colleague, I
encourage you to give it to them directly; if you're uncomfortable doing
so, let's chat and I can either get the feedback to them or we can
figure out a way to deliver it that makes you comfortable.

### One on ones

We'll do 1-1 once a week, for usually 30 minutes. It is your time. I'll
probably come up with some updates or information, but it is a time to
discuss whatever you want. The 30 minute per week is just a rule, we can
adjust it depending on your need. It can be more time or more often,
it's up to you. If you need to talk, let's do it!

### Performance

I will give you feedback on how you're doing. If I'm worried about your
performance, I will let you know. My goal is for you to know if you're
meeting the expectations (and how I think you're performing). If you
ever feel unsure about either of these things, please let me know. If
you feel like you're under performing, let's talk about it. Keep it mind
I'm here to give you all the tools to perform.
